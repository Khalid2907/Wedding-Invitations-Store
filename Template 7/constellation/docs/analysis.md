## 2. Structural Paradigm Shift

Template 7 transitions from standard vertical web scrolling into an **interactive celestial voyage**:
- **Chapter 1: The Sky & Constellation Calligraphy**: Shooting star entrance with stardust particles connecting into Arabic monogram calligraphy (`محمد` & `رحمة`).
- **Chapter 2: Our Stars**: Celestial couple portraits enclosed in glowing stardust halos.
- **Chapter 3: Our Story**: Milestones rendered as orbiting planets; clicking a planet triggers space navigation travel.
- **Chapter 4: Solar System Countdown**: Time units orbiting around the wedding date sun.
- **Chapter 5: Galaxy Gallery**: Floating photo star nodes connected by constellation lines.
- **Chapter 6: Lunar Sanctuary Venue**: Glowing moon venue section with calendar export.
- **Chapter 7: Cosmic RSVP ("Send a Star")**: Submitting an RSVP releases a glowing stardust particle into the night sky to create a guest constellation.
